What Is Affiliate Marketing?

A simple beginner's guide to understanding the affiliate business model.

Affiliate Marketing Explained

Affiliate marketing is a business model where you promote a product or service using a special tracking link.

When someone purchases through your affiliate link, the company may pay you a commission according to its affiliate program terms.

How It Works

1. Choose a product

Find a product or service that has an affiliate program.

2. Get your affiliate link

The company gives you a unique link that tracks referrals.

3. Promote it

You can share useful content through platforms such as TikTok, YouTube, websites or messaging communities, depending on the platform rules.

4. Someone buys

If a person purchases through your tracked link, the affiliate program may credit you with a commission.

Example

You create a TikTok explaining a digital marketing concept. Someone wants to learn more, visits your affiliate link and purchases the product.

Important Reality

Affiliate marketing is not guaranteed income. Results can depend on the product, audience, content, traffic, trust, consistency and many other factors.

What's Next?
