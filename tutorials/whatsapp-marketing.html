Why Conversations Matter

People often have questions before buying something. Useful conversations can help them understand what you're offering.

Three Principles

1. Don't spam. Contact people who have shown interest.

2. Provide value. Answer questions honestly.

3. Be transparent. Clearly explain affiliate relationships and offers.

Build Trust

Marketing isn't only about getting clicks. Long-term relationships are built through useful information, honesty and consistency.
